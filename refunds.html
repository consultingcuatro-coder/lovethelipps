Refunds

Refunds, not returns

Everything we sell touches lips or skin, so nothing comes back to us — not opened, not sealed, not ever. That doesn’t mean you’re stuck with it.

The Chapped-Lips Guarantee

If it doesn’t work on your lips, it’s on us: full refund, no return needed. Reply to your order confirmation email within 30 days of arrival, tell us what your lips thought, and we’ll send the money back. You keep the tube. This covers the wetlipps lip line — The Balm Stick and The Gloss Wand. Everything else we sell is covered just below.

Not happy for another reason?

Wrong shade, not what you pictured, more peppermint than you bargained for — reply to your order confirmation email within the same 30 days and we’ll set it right with a replacement or a refund. Our call, but we’ll tell you which before anything moves, and nothing gets mailed back either way.

Why nothing comes back

Once a tube has been opened, or ridden around in a pocket for a week, it can’t go to anyone else — and we wouldn’t want it to. So: no returns, opened or sealed. A refund never depends on one, and if we send you a different shade, the first one stays with you. Save yourself the postage.

Damaged or lost on the way

That’s on us too — a replacement or a refund, your pick, nothing mailed back. When to write and what helps are under Shipping.

The details

The 30 days runs from the day tracking says it arrived — or, if it never did, from the day it shipped. A full refund means everything you paid, sent back the way you paid; we send it within a few days of saying yes, and your bank decides how fast it lands. Nothing to print, pack, or ship.

The short version

We’d rather send your money back than have you quietly decide we’re just another balm.
