Our story

We’re the Lipps. Yes, really.

A married couple, four kids, one crowded Santa Barbara kitchen — and the lip care company that was hiding in our last name the whole time.

The big photo goes hereYou two — candid, laughing, golden hour on the bluffs. This is the first thing people should see: the marriage behind the brand.

How it started

Our life is outside. Our lips knew it.

AND

We live in Santa Barbara and we live outside — hiking, beach days, four kids in the sun, every day. Chapped lips were the constant, and we tried everything on every shelf.

BUT

Nothing lasted. Most of it was wax and marketing — and the marketing was better than the wax.

THEREFORE

We kept looking until we found the real thing — organic, food-grade honest, tested on all six of us. It worked. Then friends started asking. Then the name became unavoidable.

What we believe

Fifteen years in, still flirting.

We don’t think family values and attraction are opposites — we think they’re roommates — at our house they share a kitchen, albeit a very messy one. Our shelf carries both: gloss for the daily kiss, and a quieter line for the unhurried evening.

Everything with our name on it celebrates connection inside committed love — never conquest, never clinical, never embarrassed.

The house rules

Three things we won’t bend.

Connection first

We exist to help people stay close — to their person, their family, their promises. If a product doesn’t serve that, we don’t sell it.

Honesty you can eat

Every ingredient on every label in plain type. If we wouldn’t let our kids eat it, it doesn’t touch your lips.

Generosity built in

Ten percent of profits goes to restoration — not when we’re big, not someday. From the first tube.

The Restoration Fund

Restoration is possible.

We celebrate healthy love. And we know not every story has been gentle. So ten percent of our profits — tracked openly, printed on every pack, always — will fund organizations that help people find restoration after life’s hardest seasons — some chosen, some not.

10%, from day oneOf profits, not “proceeds from select items.” The math will be public.
Restoration partnersWe’re choosing organizations doing long-term rebuilding work. Partners announced as agreements finalize.
On every pack“Make restoration possible” isn’t a campaign. It’s the second line of who we are.

“This company is our date night that got out of hand.”

— the Lipps
