Our story

We’re the Lipps. Yes, really.

A married couple, four kids, one crowded Santa Barbara kitchen — and the lip care company that was hiding in our last name the whole time.

How it started

It started on a hike.

AND

On a hike, one of us was battling chapped lips — again. Then it clicked: we’re the Lipps family. We should have wet lips, not dry ones.

BUT

But why stop at balm? A family named Lipps could help with every kind of kiss.

THEREFORE

So we put our name on both: honest lip care for every day, and sliplipps for the fun, intimate moments.

What we believe

Fifteen years in, still flirting.

We don’t think family values and attraction are opposites — we think they’re roommates. Our shelf carries both: gloss for the daily kiss, and wet for fun, intimate moments.

Everything with our name on it celebrates connection inside committed love.

The house rules

Read our Lipps.

Connection first

We exist to help people stay close — to their person, their family, their promises. If a product doesn’t serve that, we don’t sell it.

Authenticity you can eat

Every ingredient on every label in plain type. If we wouldn’t let our kids eat it, it doesn’t touch your lips.

Generosity built in

Ten percent of profits goes to restoration — not when we’re big, not someday. From the first tube.

The Restoration Fund

Restoration is possible.

We celebrate healthy love. And we know the world needs restoration. So ten percent of our profits — tracked openly, printed on every pack, always — will fund organizations that help people find restoration.

10% of profits · every order · always
